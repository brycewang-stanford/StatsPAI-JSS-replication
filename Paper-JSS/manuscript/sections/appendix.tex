\section{All parity modules}\label{app:parity}

Table~\ref{tab:track-a-parity} prints every one of the
\RParityModuleCount{} parity modules (Track A), with the worst observed gap
across its headline rows against its \proglang{R} reference and, for
\StataParityModuleCount{} of them, its \proglang{Stata} reference. The gaps
cover \emph{point estimates only}; every SE row of a passing module is gated
separately (Section~\ref{sec:adjudication}). The per-row results, with notes
on convention, delegation, and the measured reason a \proglang{Stata} reference
is missing, are in \code{tests/r\_parity/results/parity\_table\_3way.md} of the
archive.

\input{tables/appendix_b_parity.tex}

\noindent \RParityStrictCount{} modules are graded T2. Their registered
tolerance is \(10^{-6}\), except that modules 18, 26, 27, 29 and 64 allow
\(2\times10^{-4}\) because the reference's optimizer stops short; the table
shows the gaps actually observed. Some passes rest on a common specification
(pre-trends at \pkg{pretrends}' 1.96 cutoff) or shared folds (DML), and every
other row above \(10^{-6}\) is in the register of
Section~\ref{sec:adjudication}, such as module 74's \proglang{Stata} gap and module 76, whose default
\proglang{Stata} rows sit up to \(1.4\times10^{-4}\) away (``see text''). The
forest row is one draw per engine (\textbf{S}). The classical-SCM row is the
disclosed disagreement (\textit{GAP}). A dagger marks the two modules that wrap a third-party \proglang{Python}
library (Section~\ref{sec:track-a}).
